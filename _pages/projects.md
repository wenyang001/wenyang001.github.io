**Hyundai–NTU–A\*STAR Corporate Lab — Foundation Models for Industrial Perception** · _Nov 2025 – Present_

Building the perception stack for reliable robotic part picking at HMGICS: open-world object detection and segmentation, 6D pose estimation, and grasp pose generation for cluttered industrial scenes. Also developing foundation-model-based few-/zero-shot anomaly detection, adapting CLIP and DINOv3 to spot defects in unseen part categories without target-domain training data. [[ACM MM 2026]](/publications/)

---

**Schaeffler–NTU Corporate Lab, Phase III — Multimodal Human–Robot Interaction** · _Jun 2025 – Nov 2025_

Benchmarked multimodal 3D human pose estimation and developed motion-retargeting methods for human–robot collaborative tasks in industrial environments.

---

**DSO National Laboratories — Image Watermarking & Multimedia Security** · _Dec 2024 – Jun 2025_

Built a robust encoder–decoder framework for image watermarking with tamper resistance under JPEG compression and noise. Proposed SSH-Net, a self-supervised hybrid network for noisy watermark removal without paired training data. [[JVCIR 2025]](/publications/)

---

**Schaeffler–NTU Corporate Lab, Phase II — Open-Vocabulary Industrial Vision** · _Mar 2024 – Dec 2024_

Developed deep learning solutions for object detection and human–object interaction (HOI) recognition in industrial environments, including open-vocabulary methods and cross-level HOI distillation from multimodal LLMs. [[KBS 2025, ACM MM 2025, EMNLP 2024]](/publications/)

---

**National Research Foundation, Singapore (NRF) — Image & Video Restoration from Damaged Storage** · _Jan 2021 – Mar 2024_

Designed learning-based frameworks to restore corrupted images and videos from damaged flash memory. Work covers JPEG bitstream restoration, bitstream-corrupted video recovery, and multimedia file forensics. [[CVPR 2023, NeurIPS 2023, IEEE TMM 2024, DCC 2023]](/publications/)
