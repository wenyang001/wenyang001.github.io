intro: |
  I am a **Research Fellow** at the Hyundai–NTU–A*STAR Corporate Lab, Nanyang Technological University, working with [Assoc. Prof. Wai Kin (Adams) Kong](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/AdamsKong/). My research develops foundation-model-based computer vision and multimodal AI for visual content understanding and industrial perception, with current work on few-/zero-shot anomaly detection, open-vocabulary detection and segmentation, 6D pose estimation, and robotic grasping at HMGICS.

  I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from NTU, advised by [Prof. Lap-Pui Chau](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/eee/people/academic-staff-and-teaching-staff/prof-chau-lap-pui/) (IEEE Fellow) and [Assoc. Prof. Kim-Hui Yap](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/entities/person/Yap-Kim-Hui). My doctoral research covered corrupted-media recovery, lightweight image super-resolution, and file-fragment understanding. I have authored **21 publications** with **340+ citations** (h-index 8), including four CCF-A first-author papers, and filed an industrial perception patent. I review for CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, and ACM Multimedia, and hold an M.Eng. and a B.Eng. from Chongqing University.
